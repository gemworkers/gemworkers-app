## What GemWorkers is
- ONE brand (house brand), not a marketplace. Buyers buy from GemWorkers (merchant of record).
- Sellers (Zakaria's brothers in Thailand/Pakistan + NL) are suppliers behind the brand. Buyers NEVER see seller names, emails or identity. Origin shows only as "Ships from {country}". Staff replies always show as "GemWorkers".
- Chosen model: "option 3" = one brand + curated maker stories (planned) + buyer questions routed directly to that stone's seller inbox (owner sees all).
- Money: Stripe (regular, not Connect) → GemWorkers; sellers paid manually (Wise / Payoneer). Per-seller commission rate, NULL = 0%. payout = price − round(price × rate, 2).
- The NL seller ("My Shop") IS Zakaria / GemWorkers itself: NL stones are GemWorkers' OWN stock (own purchases → purchase invoices, import VAT reclaim, cost_price), no payout, no commission, no seller statement. Only the brothers (TH / PK) are outside sellers.
- Legal model for the brothers' stones (Zakaria 2026-10-24): GemWorkers does NOT buy their stock — they keep ownership and GemWorkers sells their stones in its OWN name for a commission ("commissionair", EU VAT art. 14(2)(c) / 7:76 BW-style commission agreement). For VAT that counts as GemWorkers buying from the seller and reselling to the buyer, so VAT on the FULL price (as built). The seller payout is GemWorkers' (deemed) purchase cost. Needs: written commission agreement per seller, a self-billing payout statement per seller (sales, commission, payout, refunds), accountant to confirm income-tax treatment (gross vs net) and whether the NL seller is VAT-registered. Never say GemWorkers "buys from" the sellers.
- One row = one physical item (inventory_items). Don't break it.
- No live payments before the accountant has reviewed VAT/customs/merchant-of-record. This gate does not move.
