## Part 2 — The GemWorkers rules

### SOP 1 — Listing a stone
- Weigh and measure every stone; never estimate.
- Disclose every treatment (heated, oiled, filled, irradiated…). Unknown → write "unknown". Never "untreated" without proof.
- Photos in daylight, same background every time, plus one short video. Show inclusions honestly.
- Same price as on Etsy. No fake discounts ("was €1,377, now 60% off").
- Coral, shells, fossils, ivory, diamonds: answer the protected-materials questions truthfully.
- Never put your name, phone, shop name or Etsy link in the title, description or photos. Buyers buy from GemWorkers.

### SOP 2 — Storage and locations
- Every stone has a location in the app (e.g. "Drawer A3", "Box 2") and its SKU label on the bag.
- Stone taken out (photos, viewing)? Move it in the app and put it back in the same place.
- Once a month: compare the drawers with the app's list.

### SOP 3 — Sold somewhere else
- Sold on Etsy or in person → tap "Sold elsewhere" in the app immediately (the same hour).

### SOP 4 — Orders and shipping
- Ship within 3–5 working days after payment.
- Film the packing: the stone, sealing the bag, closing the parcel, the label.
- Always tracked shipping. Signature + insurance for the full value from €300.
- Shipping via agents (DHL, USPS… from Thailand; from Dubai for Pakistan stones):
  - The value on the agent's slip MUST match the GemWorkers invoice. Never a lower value — that is customs fraud, the insurance won't pay, the buyer can dispute. Photograph every slip.
  - Tracking number = the carrier's number (DHL / USPS …), not the agent's reference.
  - Confirm the parcel is insured for the full value from €300; keep the receipt.
- Sender name "GemWorkers". Print the invoice and put it in the parcel.
- Customs form: "Sale of goods / commercial", never "gift" or "sample". Real value, never lower. Country of origin = where the stone comes from (also when it ships via Dubai).
- Enter the tracking number in the app the same day.

### SOP 5 — Messages and offers
- Answer every message within 24 hours (also "I'll check and reply tomorrow").
- Never share phone, email, WhatsApp or other links. Never accept payment outside GemWorkers.
- Answer offers within 48 hours (accept, counter or decline).

### SOP 6 — Problems, returns and fraud
- Unhappy buyer or lost parcel: don't promise anything; tell Zakaria. Only the owner refunds.
- Fraud warning from Zakaria, or a buyer asking for another address or payment outside the shop → don't ship; ask Zakaria.

### SOP 7 — Money
- Your share = sale price − 12% service fee. Payouts after the return period (30 days after delivery); the statement shows every sale, fee and refund.

### SOP 8 — Away and security
- Away for more than 3 days → switch on Vacation mode.
- Your own login only; never share your password; keep a screen lock on your phone.

### SOP 9 — Export rules
- Follow your country's export rules for gemstones and keep the post office / agent / export receipts. (To confirm per country with the brothers.)

## Open points
